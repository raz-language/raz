# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to making participation in Forge a respectful, harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience level, or viewpoint.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Focus criticism on code and ideas, not people.
- Welcome questions and differing experience levels.
- Give and accept technical feedback in good faith.
- Respect project maintainers' decisions and community boundaries.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, sexualized conduct, deliberate disruption, or publication of private information are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers. Maintainers may edit or remove content, reject contributions, or restrict participation when necessary to protect the project community.
